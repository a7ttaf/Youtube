# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Internal UMS finance, revenue operations, sector leadership, company managers, audit viewers, and analysts. They use the platform during monthly close, reconciliation, export preparation, and issue investigation for 300+ YouTube channels.

## Product Purpose

UMS Smart Revenue Control Center collects, normalizes, reconciles, explains, and exports YouTube revenue and performance numbers. The product is a smart numbers engine first and a dashboard second: every money value must expose source, formula, confidence, permissions, and export readiness.

## Brand Personality

Precise, controlled, accountable. The interface should feel like a corporate finance command center: calm enough for repeated daily work, dense enough for serious reconciliation, and strict enough to make sensitive access visible.

## Anti-references

Do not make this a public marketing dashboard, crypto-style finance surface, decorative SaaS landing page, or casual analytics toy. Avoid generic hero sections, oversized stat cards, ornamental gradients, decorative card grids, and any UI that hides confidence, source, locks, permissions, or unresolved issues.

## Design Principles

- Numbers before decoration: revenue, confidence, source, and reconciliation state must be visible before visual flourish.
- Permission awareness: sensitive money views, raw files, exports, and graph reads should visibly respect role boundaries.
- Explainability everywhere: money cells need obvious explanation affordances.
- Close workflow first: month status, payment gaps, overrides, exports, and locking should feel like one operational flow.
- Dense but readable: support scanning, comparison, and repeated action without turning the screen into a wall of cards.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA contrast, keyboard reachable controls, visible focus states, reduced-motion-safe interactions, semantic tables, and color-blind-safe status cues that combine color with labels or icons.
